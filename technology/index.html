Meet our leadership team.

Clinical expertise. Technological innovation. Strategic vision.

Founded by clinicians with multidisciplinary expertise in medicine, technology and research, Elint Harbour Technologies brings together clinical insight, technical capability and strategic leadership to develop practical, responsible and scalable healthcare solutions.

CE

Dr Chudi Chuks-Eboka

Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

MBBS, MRCGP, MFSEM, CCFP
PGDip Specialist Dermatology · PGCert POCUS

Dr Chudi Chuks-Eboka is a family physician with over a decade of postgraduate clinical experience and multidisciplinary expertise spanning healthcare consultancy, clinical research, digital health and technology innovation.

Alongside his clinical career, he has undertaken consultancy and advisory roles with leading healthcare and technology organisations across the UK, Europe and North America, contributing to the development, clinical evaluation and implementation of emerging healthcare technologies, including artificial intelligence applications.

His experience bridges frontline clinical practice, healthcare technology, research and strategic consultancy, providing a practical understanding of the challenges facing modern healthcare systems. As Co-Founder and CEO of Elint Harbour Technologies, he leads the organisation's strategic direction, partnerships and healthcare innovation initiatives, with a focus on translating clinical insight and emerging technologies into sustainable solutions for healthcare organisations.

MI

Dr Morgan Ikponmwosa

Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

MBBS, MRCGP, DFSEM
MSc Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence

Dr Morgan Ikponmwosa is a general practitioner with several years of clinical experience and a multidisciplinary background spanning medicine, computer science and artificial intelligence.

His expertise brings together clinical practice, software engineering and emerging technologies, with a particular interest in developing and applying technological innovation and research to address complex challenges in healthcare delivery.

As Co-Founder and CTO of Elint Harbour Technologies, he leads the organisation's technology strategy, digital product development and technical innovation, overseeing the design and development of scalable healthcare technology solutions. His work focuses on bridging clinical medicine and technology, ensuring that digital solutions are grounded in real-world healthcare needs, technical robustness and responsible innovation.
